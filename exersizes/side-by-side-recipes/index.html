BANANA FRENCH TOAST MUFFINS

Prep Time: 15 Minutes                     Cook Time: 25 minutes

Total Time: 40 minutes                     Yields: 12 muffins

These banana French toast muffins are soft, golden, and packed with cozy banana bread flavor in every bite. They’re baked all at once instead of cooked slice by slice, making them perfect for meal prep and busy mornings. Warm them up and finish with maple syrup and/or powdered sugar for an easy, comforting breakfast.
